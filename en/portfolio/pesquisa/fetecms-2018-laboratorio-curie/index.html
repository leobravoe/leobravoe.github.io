Extended abstract published in conference proceedings (2018)

Laboratório Curie: a game for teaching experimental chemistry and good laboratory practice

Original title: Laboratório Curie: jogo para o ensino de química experimental e boas práticas no laboratório

Link to the paper

Paper details:

Authors Beatriz Landigraf Rios dos Santos; Maria Fernanda de Abreu Aguiar; Leonardo Bravo Estácio; Narcimário Pereira Coelho
Event VIII Feira de Tecnologias, Engenharias e Ciências de Mato Grosso do Sul (FETECMS)
Place and date Campo Grande/MS, Brazil, 5–10 November 2018
Institution Instituto Federal de Educação, Ciência e Tecnologia de Mato Grosso do Sul (IFMS) — Câmpus Nova Andradina
Role Project supervisor (Leonardo Bravo Estácio)
Keywords Educational games; Learning; Unity 3D

Abstract:

Many secondary-school students struggle to learn inorganic chemistry, especially when there are no teaching objects that illustrate the content presented in class. A game can act as a learning object, developing skills such as agility, motivation, cognitive abilities, discovery learning, coordination and memory. This work presents the development of a three-dimensional educational game for teaching inorganic chemistry and good laboratory practice, in which players handle objects as in real life. It was built with the Unity3D engine, 3D models made in 3ds Max, motion-capture animations prepared in MotionBuilder and, initially, Firebase for data storage and user authentication. The game is a tool to encourage students to understand reactions in a laboratory and the proper use of each piece of equipment.
