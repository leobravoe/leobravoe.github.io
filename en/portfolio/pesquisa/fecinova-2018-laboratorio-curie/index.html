Development of the teaching/learning scenarios and promotional website of the digital game Laboratório Curie

Original title: Desenvolvimento dos cenários de ensino/aprendizagem e site de divulgação do jogo digital Laboratório Curie

Link to the paper

Paper details:

Authors Maria Fernanda de Abreu Aguiar; Wesley Monaris Rodrigues; Bruno de Almeida Casas; Leonardo Bravo Estácio; Silvério Luiz de Sousa
Event V Feira de Ciência e Tecnologia de Nova Andradina (FECINOVA)
Place and date Nova Andradina/MS, Brazil, 2018
Institution Instituto Federal de Educação, Ciência e Tecnologia de Mato Grosso do Sul (IFMS) — Câmpus Nova Andradina
Recognition 3rd place in the Multidisciplinary category and CIARAMA Award — Awakening to Innovation
Keywords Digital games; Unity 3D; Teaching

Abstract:

Laboratório Curie is an educational digital game under development for mobile devices running Android and iOS. It aims to support the learning of experimental chemistry: players handle laboratory equipment and practise good laboratory conduct. This work continues its development in the teaching/learning process and its promotion.
