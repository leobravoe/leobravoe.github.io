Paper published in conference proceedings (2011)

Study and implementation of a GRASP-based heuristic algorithm for the maximum independent set problem

Original title: Estudo e implementação de um algoritmo heurístico baseado em GRASP para solução do problema do conjunto independente máximo

Link to the paper

Paper details:

Authors Leonardo Bravo Estácio; Selma Helena Marchiori Hashimoto; Irene Magalhães Craveiro
Event VI Simpósio de Matemática da FCT (VI SMAT) e Encontro Regional de Matemática Aplicada e Computacional (ERMAC)
Place and date Presidente Prudente/SP, Brazil, 18–21 October 2011
Institution Faculdade de Ciências Exatas e Tecnologia (FACET), Universidade Federal da Grande Dourados (UFGD)
Keywords Maximum independent set; GRASP; Metaheuristics

Abstract:

The maximum independent set of a graph has important applications, and no exact algorithm solves it satisfactorily: exact algorithms tend to have exponential complexity, while approximation algorithms tend to be polynomial. This paper presents an approximate solution based on the GRASP metaheuristic, with a constructive phase and a local search phase. Approximate algorithms do not guarantee an optimal solution but usually produce a satisfactory one. The algorithm efficiently found the maximum independent set of several test graphs; a deeper analysis is still needed to quantify its performance and complexity.
