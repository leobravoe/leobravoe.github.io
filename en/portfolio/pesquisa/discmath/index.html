Research Project — Technological Development and Innovation (2025)

Development and Evaluation of an Educational Application for Teaching Discrete Mathematics in Higher Education

Final report (PDF, in Portuguese) Paper (PDF, in Portuguese)

Project data:

Institution Federal Institute of Education, Science and Technology of Santa Catarina (IFSC) — Lages Campus
Programme Technological Development and Innovation — Call no. 04/2024/PROPPI (rolling admission)
Coordination Leonardo Bravo Estácio
Student researchers Caio Picinini Pagliosa and Eduardo Moreira de Jesus
Period 2025
Keywords Discrete Mathematics; Educational Application; Higher Education; Mobile Learning; Usability Evaluation

Summary:

Develop and evaluate an educational application for teaching Discrete Mathematics in higher education. The core problem is the weak consolidation of abstract concepts and the lack of timely feedback on exercises. The proposal covers the design and implementation of a cross-platform prototype, the evaluation of usability and quality in use, and the assessment of its impact on academic performance through pre- and post-tests with independent groups. The method organises successive cycles of building and evaluating, with usability testing and learning measurement, aiming at a validated artefact, replicable design guidelines and evidence of learning gains.

Results:

The project delivered DiscMath, a cross-platform prototype built in Flutter and organised into content and practice modules, with interactive questions, scored mock exams, support material by topic, immediate feedback and engagement features such as rooms and rankings. Usability was assessed with the System Usability Scale (SUS) among higher education students, reaching an average score of 73.75 — above the adopted acceptance threshold (SUS ≥ 68) and rated as satisfactory. The quasi-experimental study of the impact on academic performance, planned in the project, was not completed within the cycle and remains as a next step.

Dissemination:

The work “Developing DiscMath: an Application for Teaching Discrete Mathematics in Higher Education”, by Caio Picinini Pagliosa, Eduardo Moreira de Jesus, Vilma Gisele Karsburg and Leonardo Bravo Estácio, was presented as a video and a poster at the 7th “3 Minutes of Science and Technology” Showcase, part of the 22nd National Science and Technology Week, held by IFSC (Lages and Urupema campuses), on 4 November 2025.
