Abstract:

This programme was carried out by the Salvare charitable association to raise young people's awareness of their rights and duties in society, focusing on citizenship and social and cultural development, keeping them away from social vulnerability and risk, and preparing them to enter the local labour market with partner companies. Participants receive instruction related to military discipline (drill, firefighting, first aid, prevention of domestic accidents, physical education, sports and swimming), lectures on traffic, citizenship, violence against women, drugs and nutrition, and classes in Mathematics, Portuguese and Science. They also provide organisational support at social responsibility events.
