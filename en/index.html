Leonardo Bravo Estácio

Computing professor at IFSC, Gaspar Campus · MSc from the University of São Paulo

MSc in Sciences from the Graduate Program in Computer Science and Computational Mathematics at the University of São Paulo (USP). Professor of Basic, Technical and Technological Education (EBTT) in the Computing Technician and the Systems Analysis and Development programmes at the Federal Institute of Education, Science and Technology of Santa Catarina (IFSC), Gaspar Campus.

Research lines: web development and API performance evaluation; digital games and educational technologies.

Author of the book Introdução à Programação em JavaScript (in Portuguese). Coordinator, in 2025, of the research projects Benchmarking APIs across Different Programming Environments and DiscMath, an app for teaching Discrete Mathematics. Supervisor of final-year projects in digital games and web systems. The master's dissertation dealt with parallel implementations of Kaczmarz's method for tomographic reconstruction.
